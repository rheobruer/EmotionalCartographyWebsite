Who I Am

I am a certified relationship coach, self-published poet, and ever-evolving movement artist.

I am dedicated to the process of connecting the internal world, to the external one; whether it be in the form of a conversation with a lover, or a feeling being expressed through dance.

My purpose is to dig at the human experience, and to share what I have found along the way.

Creativity is my religion, and curiosity my love language.Love is my map; honesty, respect, and care — the paths I wish to take.

What I Do

Embodiment: is the first step in honesty. Getting in touch with your own nervous system and body talk is the groundwork for being able to be your authentic self.In a world where attuning to our own bodies is often treated like the last priority, I put it at the top of my list with my clients.Whether I am teaching you how to connect on a first date, how to get through a brutal conversation, or how to tell a story through dance, embodiment is a focus of our conversation.

Expression: is an integral part of both creativity and connection. One of the best parts of my job is seeing someone discover words for their feelings for the first time. Or move in a way that was foreign and yet more authentic than ever before. Expression is how we build understanding of each other, and yet so few of us were ever given tools on how to do so in a way that is both honest and non-violent. Communication is one of my greatest passions, and one of the most important tools for being human.

Evolution: is inevitable, as change is the only constant. It is just a question of how and when. It has always been important to me to evolve in such a way that I am enriching my own life, expanding my comfort zone, and deepening my integrity. The more grounded in embodiment and expression that I am, the more I am able to evolve toward my highest self.

It is my deepest honor to hold space for anyone looking to integrate these practices into their life, and through this, to witness their experiences of connection to self and other deepen.

I am here to support you in creating your own personal blueprint for life.Together we will explore your inner and outer worlds, creating a map of the places you’ve been, the places you want to go, and the most optimized roads for getting there.I am here to offer a judgment free zone as you face the ways you have contributed to your now suffering, and here to help you build new habits that move you toward the changes you say you want.I use somatic exercises to help you build relationship with your own inner knowing;may your compass become increasingly clear and direct.

The person who is aware of, and yet still repeating, faulty relationship patterns
I want to talk to the person who is exploring new realms of identity, or relationship dynamic.
